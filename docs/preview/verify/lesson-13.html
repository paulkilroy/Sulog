Particles - na and pa
The small particles na and pa are often confusing to non Filipino speakers of Waray. However, if
you learn how to correctly use them and practice them, you will not be confused.
NA -- means "now" or "already"
Na shows that the action is being done now, or that the action has already been
done.
PA -- means "still" or "yet"
Pa shows that the action is still being done, or that the action has not yet begun.
The na or pa is placed after the first full word of the sentence. (except with ka) If other particles
are also used, the na/pa always come FIRST. They are VIP Particles.
